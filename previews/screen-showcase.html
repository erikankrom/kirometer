Feature map
Implemented · local reader

Local, minimal telemetry

Reads Kiro’s cached plan usage without modifying its database. Output excludes prompts, source code, and account tokens.

Proposed · device firmware

A mascot with a pulse

At rest, the ghost floats softly and blinks at irregular intervals. Eye blinking has its own toggle, so it can remain visible when larger movements are paused. The ghost stays inside a dedicated black area. Slow blinks and Working indicator dots remain readable at the device’s four-frame-per-second refresh interval; no animation crosses the credit bars.

Proposed · device firmware

Honest about uncertainty

Activity, usage freshness, and connection are independent. Unknown activity can still have a known credit balance. Unavailable usage shows dashes; stale readings keep their age visible.

Proposed · device firmware

Touch and top buttons

Cycle status and credit views, wake the screen, and adjust brightness. The arrow controls above preview state navigation.

Design · Kiro palette

Purple, with purpose

Kiro purple #9147FF anchors the display. Red marks overage and a disconnected telemetry link; amber marks stale data; muted tones communicate a lost connection.

Proposed · device firmware

Always-visible status bar

A green Connected or red Disconnected label stays at the top left. Wi-Fi, battery percentage, and power indicators stay at the top right. The plan badge sits above usage; the bottom handle opens detailed metrics.

Hardware target

Made for the desk

Waveshare 2.16″ touch AMOLED inside the compact self-standing ghost, with accessible top buttons and rear power routing.
